CAR INSURANCE CLAIMS

To submit a car accident claim, the customer should provide:

1. A valid identification document.
2. Vehicle registration documents.
3. Photographs showing the damage to the vehicle.
4. A description of how the accident occurred.
5. A police report when required.

Car accident claims should be submitted as soon as reasonably possible after the incident.

For serious accidents involving injuries, customers should seek appropriate emergency assistance before dealing with the insurance claim.
