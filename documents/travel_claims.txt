TRAVEL INSURANCE CLAIMS

Travel insurance claims may cover eligible events such as cancelled trips, lost baggage, and certain medical emergencies while travelling.

Customers submitting a travel claim should provide:

1. A valid identification document.
2. Travel booking information.
3. Receipts or proof of expenses where applicable.
4. Supporting documents related to the incident.
5. A police report for theft or loss where required.

Customers should keep copies of relevant travel documents and receipts.
